§ 03Getting seen

The grant is the start. The goal is to make it visible

You don’t need to become a content creator. Share what’s happening, and we’ll handle the rest.

01

Post on LinkedIn and X

Announce the grant, what you’re working on, and why it matters. Tag @PrimordiaGrants so we can amplify immediately.

02

Add it to your LinkedIn profile

Add “Primordia Grant Recipient” to your experience. It signals you’re a funded researcher, not just talking about building.

03

Share updates once a month

A lab photo, a quick result, something that didn’t work. Honest and consistent beats polished.

04

Fill out the monthly check-in sheet

A few minutes each month. We’ll use it to highlight your progress across all Primordia channels.

It’s about documenting real science so others can find it, fund it, and build on it. People in this space want honest, consistent updates from researchers in the field — that’s what gets shared.

§ 04Grant essentials

The contract matters; the principle is simple

Use the funds for the approved project, communicate material changes, document the work, and share a substantial public output.

01 // Funds

Spend on the approved project

Use grant funds only for the approved project and budget. Keep expenses identifiable; retain receipts.

02 // Safety

Stay within scope

Stay within the approved research scope, location, biosafety level, and applicable legal or ethics requirements.

03 // Changes

Tell us before major pivots

Flag major changes to scope, methods, team, location, or budget. We can usually work through reasonable pivots.

04 // Reporting

Results + financials

Provide technical/results reporting and final financial accounting. Make the work understandable and useful.

05 // Open output

Make results public

Make all or a substantial portion of your results public — report, preprint, dataset, protocol, code.

06 // Publicity

Acknowledge the program

Acknowledge Primordia, ValleyDAO, and Biopunk Lab where relevant. Primordia may publicize non-confidential award info.
