§ 01Funding criteria
Within biology

A clear experimental plan with an aspiration to solve a real-world problem over time.

Lab access

Access to a local or community laboratory. We do not fund projects conducted in home labs.

Capable, credentials optional

No bio degree required, but you should be capable of designing and executing the experiment.

Open by default

Grantees share progress through short public updates and lab notes.

§ 02Application

Funded teams join a four-month program. See the grantee handbook.

In 5–7 sentences: what problem are you addressing? What are you testing?

What exactly will you do during the grant period? Methods, model system, assay, or computational approach.

How will you know if this worked, and what is the next experiment if it does?

Key papers, preprints, data, or links that support this approach.

What are the main milestones over the grant period?

What will the funds be spent on? Materials, equipment, services, or compute.

How will this benefit the scientific, local, or open-research community?

Who are you, and what relevant experience do you bring?
